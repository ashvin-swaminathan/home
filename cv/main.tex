\section*{Projects}

\years{2026} Bounded gaps between primes, formalized in Lean 4 --- \emph{project lead}, Axiom Math \\
A complete machine-checkable Lean 4 formalization of the theorem that $p_{n+1}-p_n \leq 246$ for infinitely many $n$, unifying Maynard's multidimensional sieve with the enlarged-simplex refinement and numerical certificate of Polymath8b. The proofs were produced with AxiomProver and released as the open-source library PrimeGapsLib.\,\, \textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\href{https://primegaps.axiommath.ai/}{site}}\,\, \textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\href{https://github.com/AxiomMath/PrimeGapsLib}{github}} \\
Press: \textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\href{https://spectrum.ieee.org/axiom-math-246-theorem-formalization}{\emph{IEEE Spectrum}}}, ``AI Used to Verify Toughest Mathematics Proof Yet'' (Benjamin Skuse, 17 August 2026); \textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\href{https://x.com/MTSlive/status/2089875266487439457}{\emph{MTS}}} interview (19 August 2026).
